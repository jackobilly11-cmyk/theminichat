What it does

Be anyone. Any species.

Pick a human face or go full fox, panda, frog, or unicorn. Your profile, your vibe — no boring defaults.

Tap to call

Jump straight from chat to a call with one tap. Clear audio, no dropped vibes, no extra apps.

Ghost delete

Delete a chat and it's truly gone. No "this message was deleted" note, no trace, no awkward questions.
